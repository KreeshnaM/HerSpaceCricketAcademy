Volunteers

Cricket doesn't happen without a community.

Every session runs because someone drove a minibus, scored a match, washed the kit, or stood in the cold counting overs. You do not need to have played to be useful here.

  • Coaches We will pay for your coaching badges.
  • Match-day volunteers Scoring, umpiring, teas, boundary flags.
  • Mentors Older players looking out for younger ones.
  • Event helpers Festival days, presentation evenings, tournaments.
  • Fundraising & support Grants, sponsors, raffles, spreadsheets.
  • Lifts and logistics Getting girls to away games and back.
