Get involved

Help us give more girls the chance to play.

We're a volunteer-run academy, and there is a lot we'd like to do as we grow. Right now, we're looking for people and organizations who can help us create more opportunities for our girls.

  • Matches and tournaments Know a team or academy interested in playing against us? We'd love to arrange more matches and give our girls the chance to gain match experience.
  • A place to play We're currently working with limited space and would love to find grounds where the girls can train and play.
  • Sponsors and partners Help us access equipment, facilities and other resources to keep the academy running and reach more girls.
  • Volunteers We're always happy to hear from people who can help with coaching, logistics, photography or the day-to-day running of the academy.

Whether you can offer your time, connect us with a potential partner or help us find new opportunities for the girls, we'd love to hear from you.
