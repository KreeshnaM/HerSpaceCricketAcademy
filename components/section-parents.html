Ask Us a Question
Who can join?

Any girl can join. You do not need to live in a particular area, go to a particular school, or know anybody here already.

Does she need cricket experience?

No. Most girls arrive having never held a bat, and we plan our sessions around that. Beginners are grouped together for the first few weeks so nobody is the only one learning.

Girls who already play get stretched too - there is a harder group and a match squad.

Can I stay and watch?

Please do. Plenty of parents stay, and a good few of them ended up volunteering. There is no expectation either way - dropping her off is absolutely fine.

Is it girls only?

Our sessions are for girls, coached by a mixed team of volunteers. A lot of the girls tell us that is exactly why they were willing to try in the first place.
