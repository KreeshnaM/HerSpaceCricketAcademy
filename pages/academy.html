Training

Same time, same place,
every week.

A session is about two hours. It starts with everyone together, splits into groups, and finishes with a game.

We group by experience, so a nervous beginner is never stuck next to someone who has played for five years. Girls move up when they are ready.

Come to one session a week and you are a full member of this academy. There is no attendance table on a wall.

First time?

Turn up ten minutes early, find whoever is holding a clipboard, and say it's your first session. Someone will walk her over and introduce her to two or three people. She will not be left standing on her own.

What she'll learn

Cricket, and a few
other things.

The left-hand column is what we teach. The right-hand column is what they actually take away.

On the pitch

  • Holding a bat properly, and why it matters
  • Front-foot and back-foot shots
  • A repeatable bowling action that won't hurt her
  • Catching, throwing, backing up, sliding
  • Wicketkeeping, if she fancies it
  • Reading a game and setting a field
  • Scoring, and the laws that catch everyone out

Off the pitch

  • Being trusted with something and not dropping it
  • Failing in front of people and carrying on
  • Telling a teammate she did well, out loud
  • Disagreeing with an umpire without losing her head
  • Turning up when she does not feel like it
  • Being the loudest person on a field, if she wants
  • Leading a group of her peers
