Join as a player

Come to a session.
That's the whole process.

There are no trials and no waiting list. Email us and we will tell you which session to come to, or just turn up to the next one.

Before you ask

She does not need to have played. She does not need her own kit. She can come on her own or with a friend. She can try it once and never come back, and nobody will chase her.

Sign up to help

What we'd ask of you.

Honestly? A couple of hours, most weeks, in season. That is it.

We will pay for your [DBS check], your safeguarding course and your coaching badges if you want them. You do not have to commit to any of that to start - plenty of people help for a season first and decide later.

You will never be alone with the girls, you will always know who is in charge of a session, and you can say no to things.

Partner with us

Schools, clubs,
councils, businesses.

If you can get cricket in front of girls who have never been offered it, we want to hear from you.

  • Schools — taster sessions, after-school clubs, a route into a real team
  • Clubs — shared pitches, fixtures, coach swaps
  • Councils & funders — participation projects and grant-funded programmes
  • Businesses — sponsor a squad, fund kit, send a team of volunteers for a day

We are a small volunteer group, so we will be straight with you about what we can and cannot deliver.
